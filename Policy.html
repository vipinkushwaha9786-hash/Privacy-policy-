2. Information We Collect

To provide account-based and online multiplayer functionality, ChessMaster may collect and store information such as:

  • Username or display name.
  • Account login information.
  • Email address, if required or provided during account registration.

3. Online Multiplayer

ChessMaster provides online multiplayer features that require communication between your device and our game servers.

During an online game, the server may process information necessary to operate the game, including:

  • Player identities.
  • Game room or match information.
  • Chess moves.
  • Game state.
  • Game result.
  • Chess clock information.
  • Draw offers and resignations.
  • Reconnection and multiplayer session information.

Information may be used to:

  • Provide online multiplayer chess.
  • Match players with opponents.
  • Save game history and results.
  • Calculate and maintain player ratings.
  • Provide leaderboards and tournaments.
  • Maintain player profiles.
  • Prevent cheating, abuse and unauthorized activity.
