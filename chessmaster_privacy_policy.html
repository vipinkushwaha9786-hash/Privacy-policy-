Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChessMaster – Play Chess handles information when you use the game.

1. Developer & Contact

ChessMaster – Play Chess is developed and maintained by the developer associated with this application.

For privacy questions, support requests, or data-related requests, contact:

vipinkushwaha9786@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

The current version of ChessMaster is designed to provide core chess gameplay without requiring an account or asking for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, microphone, camera, or other sensitive personal information.

Game statistics and settings may be stored locally on your device, such as games played, wins, rating, streak, and gameplay preferences. This local information is used to provide features within the app and is not intended to be transmitted to us by the current offline version.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Save gameplay statistics and preferences.
  • Maintain local rating and game progress.
  • Provide chess gameplay and related features.
  • Remember settings between sessions.

4. Online Services and Third Parties

The current offline version does not require an online account or transmit local game statistics to a ChessMaster server.

If future versions add online multiplayer, authentication, advertising, analytics, cloud synchronization, payments, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy and the app's data disclosures will be updated before those services are used.

5. Data Security

We aim to protect information handled by ChessMaster. Because the current version stores gameplay information locally on your device, the security of that locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game statistics and settings remain on your device until they are removed by the app, cleared through your device/app settings, or the application data is otherwise deleted.

If you have a privacy or data-deletion question, contact us at the support email above.

7. Children's Privacy

ChessMaster is a general chess game. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children through the current offline version.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Permissions

The current offline game does not need access to contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, SMS, or call information to provide its core chess functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
